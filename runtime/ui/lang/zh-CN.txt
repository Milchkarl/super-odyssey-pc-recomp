Shaders ||| 着色器
Memory use ||| 内存占用
Normal builds the shaders of everything an area loads ahead of time, for fewer stutters (about 9 GB of RAM with an NVIDIA GPU; the default). Low builds each shader the first time it is drawn: a short stutter the first time, about 6.5 GB. Auto picks Low on a PC with less than 12 GB of RAM. The game itself needs about 3.5 GB, so 4 GB of RAM is not enough. Takes effect at the next start. ||| “普通”会预先生成场景加载的所有着色器，减少卡顿（NVIDIA GPU 下约占 9 GB 内存；默认值）。“低”在每种着色器首次绘制时才生成：首次会有短暂卡顿，约占 6.5 GB。“自动”在内存少于 12 GB 的 PC 上选择“低”。游戏本身约需 3.5 GB，因此 4 GB 内存并不够用。下次启动时生效。

Preload area shaders ||| 预载场景着色器
Builds the shaders of everything an area loads right away, to reduce stalls and objects popping in. Uses a lot of memory (the graphics driver keeps them), so it stops at a quarter of your RAM or 8 GB. Shaders the game has drawn before are built ahead either way. Applies to areas loaded from now on. ||| 立即生成场景加载的所有着色器，以减少卡顿和物体突现。占用大量内存（由显卡驱动保存），因此上限为内存的四分之一或 8 GB。无论何种设置，游戏已绘制过的着色器都会预先生成。仅对之后加载的场景生效。
Asynchronous shader building ||| 异步着色器生成
On: when something is drawn before its shader is ready, the game keeps running and the object appears a moment later (or the picture is held for a frame) - no stutter. Off: nothing is ever missing, but the game pauses while the shader builds (a short hitch). ||| 开：当某物在其着色器就绪前被绘制时，游戏继续运行，物体稍后出现（或画面停留一帧）——不会卡顿。关：不会缺少任何东西，但着色器生成期间游戏会暂停（短暂顿挫）。
Warm-up at start ||| 启动时预热
How long the game spends at start getting the pipelines it has seen ready, so effects don't stutter the first time they appear. Normal: up to 15 s and 3 GB of memory. Long: up to 30 s and 12 GB (at most half of this PC's memory). Auto: Long with 16 GB of memory or more. Applies at the next start. ||| 游戏启动时花多久将已见过的管线准备好，避免特效首次出现时卡顿。普通：最多 15 秒和 3 GB 内存。长：最多 30 秒和 12 GB（最多为这台电脑内存的一半）。自动：内存 16 GB 及以上时为“长”。下次启动时生效。
Compile shaders ||| 编译着色器
Compiles the game's shader archives and pipeline states recorded during play at the next start. A progress window shows both passes. Use after a driver update or after visiting new areas. ||| 在下次启动时编译游戏着色器存档以及游玩期间记录的管线状态。进度窗口会显示两个阶段。在更新驱动或探索新区域后使用。

Smooths jagged edges of the game image before it is scaled to the window. SMAA: finds the edges and blends only along them, so textures stay sharp (the better look). FXAA: one quick pass that softens every high-contrast edge - the lightest on the GPU, a little softer. Most useful at a lower internal resolution. (MSAA isn't offered: the game lights its scene in several full-screen passes after the geometry, where MSAA has nothing to work on - a higher internal resolution, 2x and up, is the supersampling that works.) ||| 在游戏画面缩放到窗口之前平滑锯齿边缘。SMAA：检测边缘并仅沿边缘混合，纹理保持清晰（效果更好）。FXAA：一遍快速处理，柔化所有高对比度边缘——GPU 负担最轻，画面稍柔。在较低内部分辨率下最为有用。（不提供 MSAA：游戏在几何体绘制之后通过多遍全屏处理进行场景光照，MSAA 在其中无处发挥——更高的内部分辨率（2x 及以上）才是有效的超采样手段。）
