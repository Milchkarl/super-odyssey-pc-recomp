## New

- **Native HDR** with scene colour grading (peak and paper-white brightness, exposure, highlights, shadows, contrast, saturation and more), on the Display tab.
- **Graphics card choice** on the Display tab, for PCs with more than one.
- **Field of view** slider (100-150%) on the Display tab.
- **Bloom quality High** now draws the glow at the picture's resolution (#102).
- **Shadow edges: Soft** on the Graphics tab.
- **Switch 2 controllers over Bluetooth** (Pro Controller 2, Joy-Con 2, with gyro and HD rumble), Windows only, off by default: Controls > Switch 2 controllers. Contributed by zigzagulouszagger (#150).
- **A new settings menu**: dropdowns, switches and sliders, memory bars for the graphics choices, and the Controls page in the same style.

## Fixed

- **Shadow distance** now changes how far shadows reach (#135).
- **Mario shivering in the sun in the Moon Kingdom** (#124).
- **Crash where a video plays** on installs without the game's videos (#124).
- **Mouse pointer over cutscenes** (#109).
- **Menu navigation**: Down reaches everything on a page, and Up goes back to the page's own tab.
- **Spin throw key**: Mario no longer drifts, and the spin comes out every time (#154).
- **Mouse buttons bound to A or B** act as A or B in the menus and on the map (#151).
- **"can't load odyssey_game.dll (error 193)" at start** (#152).
- **RecompLauncher Easy Setup** shows its progress and errors (#144).
- **Out of memory on small integrated GPUs** (#147).
- **Crash on Intel graphics** when depth clamping turns on (#122, #148).
- **Linux**: a Japanese font for the game's system text where one is installed (#146).
- **Untranslated texts** in the quit dialog, the setup page and the on-screen keyboard (#153).

From now on, updates will be released weekly to maintain the project.

Thank you for reporting all the issues and for the logs.
