## Fixed / new

- **Death screen (#114)**: the screen now stays black from the closed wipe to the coin count, instead of showing the scene again.
- **Stew and sea (#116, #117)**: the game could lose the surface of Luncheon's stew and Seaside's sea for a whole visit, so a captured Lava Bubble fell through and water acted like land. If you still see either, please tell us where.
- **HUD and menu quality** (Graphics, Switch / High): High draws the HUD and menus with crisp, smooth edges on big screens. It replaces "Sharper HUD and menus".
- **HUD in 16:10 and 4:3** (Steam Deck included): the counters, life gauge and button guides now sit at the picture's edges.
- **Box under the life gauge** after changing the aspect ratio: fixed.
- **Frame rates above 60 on game 1.4.1** (experimental): Mario, Cappy and the camera now move between game updates, so motion is much steadier at 120 and 144.
- **60 FPS with VSync off** ran slightly fast (60.6); it is now exactly 60.
- **Low-end PC preset** (Performance): one button for lighter settings on slow PCs.
- **RecompLauncher**: the game's log is now written when started from the launcher.

## What's next

Fixing leftover bugs and improving the settings and the settings menu. The port is pretty much complete at this point, so the road to 1.0.0 will follow players' experience: please keep the reports coming.
